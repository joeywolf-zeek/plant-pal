👆 The finger test: how to tell if a plant needs water

Push a finger into the soil up to the first knuckle (about an inch / 2–3 cm).

  • Dry and crumbly? Water thoroughly until it drains out the bottom, then tap Watered.
  • Cool or damp, or soil sticks to your finger? Wait — tap Still damp and you'll be reminded in 2 days.

Schedules are estimates. The soil is always the boss — more plants die from too much water than too little.
